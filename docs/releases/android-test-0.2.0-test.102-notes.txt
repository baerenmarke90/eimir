eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.102

- When a Transfer fails, the app now names the reason, and large exports are split into parts that stay within the size limit.
- Pull to refresh now draws a small disc that follows your finger and springs back smoothly on release.
- Swiping between tabs is smoother, with less jank on the tabs and on the map, and gives a single haptic tick per swipe.
- Tapping a main tab now opens it at the top.
- The partner map shows when each location was last updated.
- Diary pages show their paper details more cleanly, and album map markers are simpler.
- Discover story controls fade out after a short moment so the picture stays clear.
- Push notification setup now shows its progress while it activates.
- Lists and Notes use the same header at the top as Momente and Planen, and title-only pages have an even gap below the title.
- On Wir, the light stays visible in Light mode, and the Zu zweit design preview no longer collapses in the picker.
