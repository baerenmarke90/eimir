eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.96

- Motion is now consistent across the whole app: sheets, dialogs, pages, Wir, Momente, Planen, the map, the recap film, stories, the garden and the games share one calm set of movements, and nothing replays when you return to a tab.
- Wir opens more calmly, the "Wie geht's?" line shows again each app session until you have checked in, avatar photos no longer flicker, and the rings breathe smoothly.
- On your anniversary, a birthday or another shared important day, Wir greets you once that day with a small celebration around the heart.
- Swiping between tabs, dragging a sheet away, pull to refresh, reordering lists and zooming photos follow your finger more naturally and can be caught mid-movement.
- New haptic feedback for gestures, the check-in and gestures sent to your partner, with an on/off switch in Settings > Appearance.
- The garden runs more smoothly, the Momente map opens in one piece and remembers the place you last selected during the session, and idle pages stop animating.
- "Reduce motion" is respected everywhere, including the map's zoom buttons and panning.
- Detail pages slide in from the side, and the fixes from the test.95 device check are included (idle motion, scrolling, layout, dialogs and copy).
- Private surfaces are protected against screenshots, once messages open in the app only, and a failed push setup now shows its cause.
- The app starts faster: Wir loads first and the rest of the app, including the legal texts, loads in the background right after.
- Account deletion now also removes sent and received partner gestures, and imported videos are checked like uploads.

The product changes come from main at 5b377a541c79146764c02b39322db0ed1916afe2.
Install over test.95 with the same de.eimir.app package, signing certificate and test server. This build uses versionCode 96.
