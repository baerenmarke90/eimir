eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.91

- Zu zweit and Handschrift are now available as personal app designs, with their own names and note layouts.
- The monthly recap is now cut as a short film from your shared photos, clips and answers, and the year recap plays as a film of month chapters.
- Each recap film gets its own look and staging, chosen from your photos, so consecutive recaps do not repeat.
- Finished recaps are now announced, and late entries are recognised and included.
- The thinking-of-you ripples now end with their flight instead of lingering.

The product changes come from main at 3d57405ea5150f85b7b80ed028a7dd6e644ebf67.
Install over test.90 with the same de.eimir.app package, signing certificate and test server. This build uses versionCode 91.
