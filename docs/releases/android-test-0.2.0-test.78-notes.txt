eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.78

- Read a milestone like a memory: back button, a written-out Edit action, the date with its relative time, the milestone text on a keepsake surface and the comment field right below.
- See the heart moment notes on Erinnerungen with their emotion, in the same style as the diary.
- Milestones in "Eure Geschichte" on Wir are now green dots of the same size as the others.
- Wir, Momente, Planen and Mehr share one title size, typeface and position, and the same distance to their menus.
- Browse the Karte tab with a taller map and compact place rows, without an add button, with an icon-only fullscreen button, and use the fullscreen map in landscape.
- See profile photos instead of initials in the Glücksmomente and Zeitreise histories.
- The add button in the bottom right stays in place when you switch tabs instead of jumping into view.
- Photos no longer carry a comment button, because the comment field is right below them.
- Controls on photos and the map step aside while you swipe or pan, and the zoom buttons no longer cover the map on touch screens.
