eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.98

- No product changes since test.97; this build republishes the same main revision with versionCode 98. It contains everything listed for test.97:
  - When an upload fails, the photo now says why (storage full, file too large, format, connection lost or stalled) and shows a short reference to report.
  - Storage settings show how much of the Space's Cloud storage is used, and an upload that no longer fits says so plainly.
  - Tapping your or your partner's avatar on the distance map zooms to that person; "Uns zeigen" brings both back.
  - A completed Plan offers "Als Erinnerung / Herzmoment / Meilenstein festhalten" in its menu at any time.
  - Wir counts down to the next upcoming Plan automatically; a Plan can be set to "Als Erstes anzeigen" or never to show.
  - Wir shows the games that are waiting for you, such as your turn or a seed that arrived.
  - Notifications on Android now say what happened (Plan time and place, reminder, list and note titles, excerpts); Android's lock-screen setting decides what is visible there. A seed's words and surprises stay hidden.
  - In the garden, the messenger's flight is brought into view when you send a seed or a bouquet, and "Eure Gartengeschichte" at the bottom tells what happened in your garden.
  - During a Wish detective game at a distance, the partner's wishes are hidden in the Wishes tab until they are revealed.
  - Importing a Transfer Bundle explains what happens in words, and skipped videos can be recovered.
  - The Date-Abend countdown on Wir now ticks in place and only while it is visible, instead of fading again every second.
  - The Wir opening runs as one calm sequence on a single clock, so late data (place, quote, check-in, photo) joins it or appears at rest.
  - Importing the same Transfer Bundle again no longer duplicates content.
  - Videos in a Transfer Bundle now count as video uploads, and videos that were skipped can be recovered by importing the same file again.
  - Photos imported from a Transfer Bundle are checked like regular uploads.
  - A Transfer import or an upload that would exceed the Cloud storage quota is now refused.

The product changes come from main at 4c011480db2a1f64ec34c36406b67ea508fd8480.
Install over test.97 with the same de.eimir.app package, signing certificate and test server. This build uses versionCode 98.
