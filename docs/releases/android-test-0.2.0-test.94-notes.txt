eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.94

- Find shared lists and notes in the new Lists tab. Tick off entries from the overview, and use the three-dot menu to edit or delete an item.
- Wir opens more gently, shows location on the names line and now welcomes people in a new, empty Space with invitations and a shortcut to their first moment.
- Open the garden in full screen and landscape on Android. Watering after dark now shows a moonbow.
- Play Who of Us, Wish Detective and Our Moments together from separate phones. Wish Detective now needs one open wish per person.
- Give finished Picture Ping-Pong and Heart Hunt moments a name, and take a private photo directly from a one-time message.
- Your theme, diary layout and selected garden and Wir preferences follow your account across devices.
- Get a notification for a newly shared note and open it directly. The notification preview keeps the note text private.
- Discover a new daily catalog of 1,000 sayings across ten categories.
