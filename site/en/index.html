For couples

Your home for what connects you.

eimir. is a private app for two. You keep memories, collect wishes and plan together.

Open the appLearn more

Runs in the browser, also as a PWA without an app store. Or self-hosted.

Structure

Three areas

At the bottom of the app you switch between Us, Moments and Plans. Profile and settings are under “More”.

Us

Your starting point

Shows your time together, the mood and battery level of you both, a quote and a moment from your story.

Moments

Memories and diary

Collect memories with photos and text, keep your diary and see your places on the map.

Plans

Plans, wishes, occasions

Collect wishes, plan things to do and add occasions. If you cannot agree, “What shall we do?” helps.

Highlights

Four features in detail

Diary

The diary

Entries sit on a timeline sorted by month, with photo, date and place. You group related periods into chapters.

  • Photos, notes and moods per entry
  • Chapters such as “Our summer” bundle several entries
  • The same entries also appear on a map
Garden

The shared garden

Each of you can pick a seed for the other once a day. If you are both there, the garden grows a little.

  • Send seeds, optionally with a few words
  • Plants, seasons and small visitors
  • The garden book records what has grown
Recaps

Recaps

Your moments turn into monthly and yearly recaps. You page through them or watch them as a sequence.

  • Adjust the selection and add your own words
  • Earlier months and years stay available
Our story

Our story

Shows since when you have been together and how many days that is. Below it, you see what is coming up next.

  • Countdown to the next anniversary, birthday or plan
  • Overview per year, for example “Our year 2025”

Access

With an app, without one, or self-hosted

You decide how to use eimir.

In the browser

Open app.eimir.de and sign in. It works on a smartphone and on a computer, without installing anything.

As a PWA, no app store

If you do not want to install an app: choose “Add to Home Screen” in the browser. eimir. then starts like an app in its own window. You still need a connection to the server.

Self-hosted

eimir. is also available as a self-hosted version for personal, non-commercial use. It runs with Docker Compose on your own server and the data stays there. You need a server and some admin experience.

Security

What happens to your data

Just for two

A space belongs to two people. There is no feed and there are no followers.

Stored encrypted

Sensitive content and photos are stored encrypted in the database and storage. The keys are managed separately. Transfer uses TLS.

Private stays private

Only the person who created them sees private notes and gift ideas, not even the partner.

Export and self-hosting

You can export your content. If you want the data entirely with you, run eimir. yourself.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an app?

No. eimir. runs in the browser at app.eimir.de. On a smartphone you can add it to the home screen as a PWA, and it then starts like an app, without a store.

Is eimir. a social network?

No. There is only your shared space, no feed and no followers.

How is my data protected?

Transfer uses TLS. Sensitive content and media are stored encrypted, with the keys kept separate from the database and storage.

Is it end-to-end encrypted?

No. The server decrypts content to display it, search it and build recaps from it. If you want full control over the server, run eimir. yourself.

Can my partner see my private notes?

No. Only the person who created them sees private notes and gift ideas.

Can I take my content with me?

Yes. You always keep a way to export your content and photos.

Can I run eimir. myself?

Yes, as a self-hosted version for personal, non-commercial use. You need your own server with Docker, and the operating guide comes with the project.
