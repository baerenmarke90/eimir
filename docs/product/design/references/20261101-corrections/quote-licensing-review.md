# Quote catalog purchasing review

Checked November 1, 2026. Research only: no purchase, subscription, provider integration or outbound inquiry.

The existing `daily.quote` Pro capability uses a curated catalog with traceable rights. The provider must supply content rights, not merely paid access to an endpoint. Normal users must not configure credentials; any future provider runs behind the existing operator/backend boundary.

| Candidate | Verified offer | Assessment for eimir. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [STANDS4 / Quotes.net](https://www.quotes.net/quotes_api.php) | Explicitly offers a premium commercial API license by contacting the provider; no public commercial price. | A concrete commercial-licensing lead. German coverage, rights in individual quotations/translations, permitted storage and Cloud/Self-Hosted distribution are not established by the public offer. Request a written proposal before selecting it. |
| [They Said So](https://theysaidso.com/api) | Premium API: USD 9.99/month, 2,500 calls/day; Ultra USD 24.99/month, 8,000/day; Enterprise USD 74.99/month, advertised unlimited calls/day. | Paid transport is available, but the [terms](https://theysaidso.com/terms) say third-party quotations remain their authors'/estates' property and the service presents them under fair-use principles. This is not evidence of a transferable German content license. Do not buy it on that assumption. |

Recommended procurement direction: first obtain a written STANDS4 proposal for a small, curated German catalog, or commission original German daily thoughts with explicit application/distribution rights. The latter is an editorial procurement option, not a verified vendor offer or price. Keep the current rights-reviewed catalog until an actual contract is accepted.

The proposal needs to cover the languages and exact text versions, source/author/translator attribution, reproduction in a paid app and Web product, local/server caching and offline presentation, commercial Cloud use, distribution with Self-Hosted installations, territory, term, withdrawal/deletion and post-termination use. A runtime API must remain optional and replaceable; no account IDs, partner names, preferences or relationship content should leave eimir. for quote selection. An approved distributable catalog fits the existing local deterministic daily selection better than a mandatory per-user external request.

Business/freemium impact reviewed: research changes no tier, entitlement or operating cost. Final licensing scope and actual cost remain unresolved until a provider quote/contract exists. No specific modern quote or bulk scraped dataset is approved by this review.
