eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.83

- Create a shopping list from the list overview or Quick Create by choosing between a plain list and a shopping list.
- Open a list entry from its name in one sheet with its title, quantity, Save, Cancel and Delete, and see a bar above the bottom navigation with the entries in your cart that opens the cart as a sheet where you can put an entry back.
- See a new list entry only once while it is being sent, instead of twice for a moment.
- See at a glance how much of a shared list is still open, such as "2 fehlen noch", with a two-tone bar that shows who finished what, and find finished entries in "Im Wagen" or "Erledigt".
- Recognize who added or finished each list entry by their initials on their own color: the creator of the list is rosé and the other person is blue, the same on both phones.
- Add an optional "Menge" to shopping list entries, and watch changes from your partner arrive on an open list within seconds.
- Close the check-in panel by tapping anywhere outside it, and read "Du teilst deine Entfernung mit …" in your own wording.
- Open the map directly from the location settings with "Auf der Karte ansehen", and zoom the map over the same area more smoothly.
