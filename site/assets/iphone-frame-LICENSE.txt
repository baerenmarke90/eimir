iPhone 18 Pro device frame (site/assets/iphone-frame.css)

Excerpt of the iPhone 18 Pro rules and the shared base rules from react-mockframe 1.3.0
(https://github.com/mbdev3/react-mockframe), which adapts Marvel devices.css
(https://github.com/marvelapp/devices.css, Copyright (c) 2014 Marvelapp, MIT License).
The frame geometry was measured by the react-mockframe author from Apple's product bezels.
Apple and iPhone are trademarks of Apple Inc. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
