eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.110

- Photos taken at the same spot no longer create several identical Places; a new photo links the Place that already exists nearby, also across different entries.
- A photo added to a Heart Moment is kept after saving, and a chosen cover stays chosen when its photo creates a Place.
- On Wir, a "New for you" notice or a "Your turn" game disappears as soon as you have opened it, without a manual refresh; section links now sit level with their titles and the gaps between modules are even.
- In the recaps, "Open original" is gone, "Add own words" appears once on the closing page, and "Adjust selection" moved into the three-dot menu.
- After saving a diary entry, Momente now shows the entries instead of only the month header.
