eimir. Android 0.2.0-test.103

- In Appearance, a new Designfarben card below Eure Farben lets you pick the design colours independently of the layout; "Passend zum Design" keeps the active design's palette.
- The Thread (Faden) and Handwritten designs are retired. A saved choice of either switches to Connected, and old Thread links open Wir.
- You can send a problem report or feedback from inside the app, from an error screen or the More page. The reply to a report arrives once as a simple notification, and your reports are included in your personal data copy.
- The in-app Transfer export and import is closed. Moving a Space is handled by the operator.
- Swiping between the main pages is smoother, follow-up touches behave consistently, and a page you swiped away keeps its own view.
- The keyboard stays open while autofill suggestions are shown.
- The app stays open when the launcher icon changes after signing in, and the launcher works on Android 8 and 9 again.
- The footer of a short task sheet stays reachable above the keyboard.
- The app starts faster because language packs now load on demand.
- Clearer German wording for the daily garden gift in Unser Garten, Wir, the garden history and the inbox, and for the matching push notifications.
